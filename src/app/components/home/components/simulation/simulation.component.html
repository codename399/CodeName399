Learn the simulation flowThis page is a diagnostic lab, not a live trading engine
1. CaptureRead the lifecycle workbook and reconstruct each tick, indicator snapshot and trade stage.
→
2. EvaluateApply the captured signal plus score, confidence, R/R, edge and spread gates at the decision point.
→
3. ReplayWalk ticks chronologically. The cursor shows exactly where the selected observation sits in the market path.
→
4. AttributeCompare accepted, missed and losing outcomes with future-price evidence to explain what happened.
→
5. LearnOptimization tests counterfactual thresholds; holdout checks whether a proposed change generalizes.
