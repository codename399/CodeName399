-----Buying flow------------
Buy method locks this stock then fetches marginal price for the stock
Then fetches lowest price change in last five 1 minute candles
Then applies this formula to get quantity 

*-------formula-----*
buyprice = (marginalprice * quantity) + buycharges
sellprice = ((marginalprice + (lowest price change in last five 1 minute candles)) * quantity) + sell charges

find quantity where sellprice - buyprice > buy charges + sell charges

then check if that much quantity is available to buy in market at current price
if quantity is available buy maximum quantity that can be bought at that price using available funds
*-------------------*
place actual buy order on platform if autotrading is enabled
subscribe the stock in websocket to monitor live ticks
place stop loss of 0.5%
mark the stock as owned
add the stock in trade order repo
update balance
if quantity is less than calculated quantity release the stock
--------------------------------

-------Selling flow-------------
use live ticks
identify when price moves above total charges, activate trailing stop
sell when trailing stop hits or stop loss hits
release the lock
remove ownership of the stock
update balance
place actual sell order on platform if autotrading is enabled
